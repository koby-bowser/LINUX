LEGEND:
───────
PV  = Physical Volume (actual disk/partition)
VG  = Volume Group (pool of PVs)
LV  = Logical Volume (virtual partition presented to OS)
PE  = Physical Extent (smallest allocation unit, default 4 MB)
LE  = Logical Extent (maps 1:1 to PE)

KEY RELATIONSHIPS:
──────────────────
• 1 VG = 1+ PVs combined
• 1 VG = 1+ LVs carved from it
• LVs can span multiple PVs (striping)
• Free space in VG = sum of free PEs across all PVs
• LVs can be resized by allocating/freeing PEs

EXAMPLE FLOW - Extending lv_app by 5 GB:
──────────────────────────────────────────
1. Check VG free space:  vgs vg_data
2. Extend LV:            lvextend -L +5G /dev/vg_data/lv_app
3. Resize filesystem:    resize2fs /dev/vg_data/lv_app
4. Result: lv_app now 15 GB, VG free space reduced by 5 GB
