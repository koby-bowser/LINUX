### The 2 Exact Reasons Why Your Browser Fails

#### 1. CORS Block (Cross-Origin Resource Sharing)
* Your browser is viewing the Centreon Web page on port **`443`** (`https://extranetclient.outremer-telecom.fr`).
* The AJAX script calls port **`9443`** (`https://map.extranetclient.outremer-telecom.fr:9443`).
* Because the port is different (`443` vs `9443`), modern web browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Edge) treat this as a **Cross-Origin request**.
* Browsers strictly **block** cross-origin AJAX calls unless Nginx returns CORS headers:
  ```nginx
  add_header 'Access-Control-Allow-Origin' '*' always;
  add_header 'Access-Control-Allow-Methods' 'GET, POST, OPTIONS' always;
  add_header 'Access-Control-Allow-Headers' 'Authorization, Content-Type' always;
  ```
  Without these headers, your browser silently rejects the response and displays **Connection test failed**.

#### 2. Workstation Hairpin NAT
* Your browser resolves `map.extranetclient.outremer-telecom.fr` to the public IP **`109.62.64.18`**.
* If your PC is connected to the internal corporate LAN/VPN, traffic from your PC targeting the corporate public VIP `109.62.64.18:9443` is dropped by the corporate firewall (lack of NAT loopback / Hairpin NAT).
